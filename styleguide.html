Style guide

Tareeq (طريق) means “road” or “way”. The look is a night drive through olive country: asphalt, a dashed gold centre line, and warm paper. Tokens live in css/tokens.css; components in css/base.css.

Logo

The mark is a winding road with a dashed centre line, on olive. It is the app icon and favicon. Pair it with the Fraunces wordmark; the Arabic طريق may be set in Cairo 800 alongside.

Clear space: one quarter of the mark's width on all sides. Minimum size: 24px. Don't recolour the road, rotate the mark, or place it on olive.

Voice and driving-safe rules

Do

Speak plainly and warmly: “Say it back.” Use short sentences. Keep to one idea per screen. Lead with the Arabic.

Don't

Don't use jargon, hype or guilt (“You missed a day!”). Don't require reading, tapping or typing while the car is moving.

Audio is the primary interface. Visual UI is for when the user is parked: large targets, high contrast, one action per screen.

Metadata and social

Primary keywords: learn Arabic, study Arabic language, Palestinian Arabic. Titles stay under 60 characters, descriptions under 160. Social images are 1200×630 (Open Graph) and 1200×600 (X card); sources are in social/; regenerate the PNGs with scripts/render-images.py.
